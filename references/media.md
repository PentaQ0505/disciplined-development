# Visual and media deliverables

Apply when changing UI visuals, screenshots, video, animation, or exported media.

- Use user-provided or explicitly approved assets and representative product states. Do not fabricate capabilities, screens, success states, export results, or privacy claims.
- Preserve the product's visual language instead of substituting a generic template. Check the actual rendered output, not just source or a success message.
- For video, make rendering reproducible by fixing the timeline, frame rate, assets, and random seeds where relevant. Render representative frames first, then inspect the full output for transitions, ending, audio sync, and delivery specifications.
- Use a new output filename by default; preserve original footage and prior QA. Do not overwrite without authorization.
- A matching still frame does not prove continuous playback is correct. Automated audio measurements do not replace listening. Report what was actually inspected.
- If a platform gives only a broad rejection reason, do not claim to know the exact offending frame. Make the smallest approved adjustment and preserve uncertainty in the report.
